Process

Every project follows
the same path

A repeatable process is what makes results repeatable. Each stage ends with a review before the next one begins.

  1. 01

    Requirements

    We define what the board must do, its limits, and how success will be checked.

  2. 02

    Feasibility & architecture

    Technical risks, component choices and cost are assessed before a proposal is issued.

  3. 03

    Design & review

    Schematic and layout are built and reviewed against a checklist, not by eye alone.

  4. 04

    Verification

    Design rules, manufacturing checks and a test plan confirm the design is ready.

  5. 05

    Handover

    Production files, BOM and documentation are delivered with a clear revision history.

Principles

How we work

Engineering first, manufacturing second

A design is judged by whether it works and can be built. Production decisions follow from the engineering, never the reverse.

Honest about limits

We state what we can deliver and what we cannot. If a project needs a partner or a capability we do not have, we say so up front.

Documented and reviewable

Decisions, revisions and test results are written down so another engineer can pick the work up without guessing.

Respect for your intellectual property

Your design is yours. Terms around ownership and confidentiality are agreed in writing before work starts.

FAQ

Questions, answered

What is a "Core"?

A Core is a specialized engineering unit inside DOT CORE, such as PCB Core. Each has its own scope, process and quality checks. New Cores open when the existing ones are stable.

Which Core can I work with today?

PCB Core is the first Core and is in its launch phase. Send an inquiry and we will confirm availability and scope for your project.

What will I receive at the end of a project?

Depending on scope: schematics, PCB layout files, manufacturing files, a bill of materials and design documentation. Exact deliverables are listed in the proposal.

Do you manufacture the boards?

Our focus is design and engineering. Fabrication and assembly are coordinated per project, and the scope is agreed in the proposal before work begins.

I already have a board that does not work. Can you help?

Yes. Debugging and repair review is a separate request type. Describe the fault and include any schematics or photos you have.

Do you work with students and startups?

Yes. See the support section above, or write to us with a short description of your project.
