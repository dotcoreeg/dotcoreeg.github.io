01 — About

About

Who we are

We design hardware that is built to be manufactured, tested and maintained — not only hardware that looks right on a screen. Engineering first. Manufacturing second.

Mission

To deliver electronics and technology that can be built, verified and supported — and to make that standard reachable for students, researchers and startups.

Vision

An internationally credible engineering company from Cairo, growing one specialized Core at a time, each held to a documented standard.

A

Engineering first, manufacturing second

A design is judged by whether it works and can be built. Production follows engineering, never the reverse.

B

Honest about limits

We state what we can deliver and what we cannot. If a project needs a partner, we say so up front.

C

Documented and reviewable

Decisions, revisions and test results are written down so another engineer can continue the work.

D

Your intellectual property

Your design is yours. Ownership and confidentiality are agreed in writing before work starts.

08 — Questions

FAQ

What is a "Core"?

A Core is a specialized engineering unit inside DOT CORE, such as PCB Core. Each has its own scope, process and quality checks. New Cores open when the existing ones are stable.

Which Core can I work with today?

PCB Core is the first Core and is in its launch phase. Send an inquiry and we will confirm availability and scope for your project.

What will I receive at the end of a project?

Depending on scope: schematics, PCB layout files, manufacturing files, a bill of materials and design documentation. Exact deliverables are listed in the proposal.

Do you manufacture the boards?

Our focus is design and engineering. Fabrication and assembly are coordinated per project, and the scope is agreed in the proposal before work begins.

I have a board that does not work. Can you help?

Yes. Debugging and repair review is a separate request type. Describe the fault and include any schematics or photos you have.

Do you work with students and startups?

Yes. See the support section, or write to us with a short description of your project.
